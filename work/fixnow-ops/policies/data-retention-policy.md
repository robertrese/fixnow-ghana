# Data Retention Policy Draft

Generated: June 21, 2026

This draft needs legal approval before production use.

## Proposed Retention

| Data | Proposed Retention | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Open reports | Until resolved plus 24 months | Civic follow-up and trend analysis |
| Closed reports | 24 months | Accountability and recurring issue analysis |
| Hidden/rejected reports | 12 months | Abuse prevention and audit |
| Anonymous reporter details | Do not collect by default | Safety and privacy |
| Optional contact details | Until case closure plus 6 months | Follow-up |
| Responder accounts | While active plus 12 months | Security and audit |
| Audit logs | 24 months | Moderation accountability |
| Backups | 30 to 90 days | Disaster recovery |

## Deletion

Users should be able to request deletion of optional contact details. Reports may be retained without identifying details when needed for public-interest infrastructure tracking.

## Review

Review retention every 6 months during pilot and annually after launch.
