# Responder Verification SOP

Generated: June 21, 2026

## Purpose

Only verified responders should access shared cases, update statuses, or view operational queues.

## Accepted Responder Types

- District Assembly desk
- NADMO representative
- Utility service desk
- Health or sanitation response desk
- Approved community response partner
- FixNow moderation team

## Required Verification

Before approving an account, collect:

- Full name
- Work phone
- Work email if available
- Agency or organization
- Region and district
- Role requested
- Verification note from supervisor or official contact

## Approval Steps

1. Responder submits onboarding request.
2. Moderator checks completeness.
3. Admin verifies agency identity by phone, official email, or signed letter.
4. Admin approves and creates account.
5. Responder must change temporary password after first login.
6. Account is reviewed after 30 days.

## Removal Steps

Disable account immediately when:

- Responder leaves role
- Account is shared
- Abuse or privacy violation is suspected
- Agency requests removal

Record reason in audit notes.
