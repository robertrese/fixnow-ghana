# Moderation Staffing SOP

Generated: June 21, 2026

## Purpose

Keep public reports useful while protecting reporters, vulnerable people, and responders.

## Roles

- Moderation Lead: owns policy, escalations, training, and quality review.
- Moderator: reviews flagged reports, hides unsafe details, escalates urgent cases.
- Admin: approves responder accounts and removes abusive accounts.
- Responder Lead: confirms agency actions and closes cases.

## Minimum Launch Staffing

- 1 Moderation Lead
- 2 trained moderators
- 1 backup admin
- Named responder contacts for pilot districts

## Moderation Rules

Hide or redact reports that include:

- Names, phone numbers, or addresses of private people
- Photos of children or vulnerable people without clear public-interest need
- Exact location for violence, intimidation, domestic safety, or retaliation risks
- Hate speech, threats, harassment, or political targeting
- Medical details that identify a person
- False or unverifiable claims that could harm someone

Escalate immediately:

- Flooding, fire, collapsed structures, exposed electrical hazards
- Violence or threats
- Reports involving children, schools, hospitals, or disabled people
- Repeated reports from the same area showing urgent danger

## Daily Workflow

1. Review new flagged reports.
2. Hide unsafe personal details.
3. Escalate urgent cases to responder contacts.
4. Close duplicate spam.
5. Sample approved reports for quality.
6. Record decisions in audit notes.

## Quality Review

Each week, the Moderation Lead reviews:

- 20 approved reports
- 20 hidden reports
- All escalated reports
- All moderator disputes

Update training when mistakes repeat.
