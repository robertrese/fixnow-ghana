# Ghana Privacy And Legal Review Checklist

Generated: June 21, 2026

This is not legal advice. A Ghana-qualified privacy/legal reviewer should approve the final policy before launch.

## Data Categories

Review whether the app collects or stores:

- Report descriptions
- Photos or media
- Approximate location
- Precise location
- Reporter contact details when provided
- Responder account details
- Moderation notes
- Audit logs
- Device or network metadata

## Required Decisions

- Lawful basis for processing reports
- Consent language for optional location and photos
- Whether anonymous reporting is allowed in all flows
- Whether sensitive reports hide exact location by default
- Data retention periods
- Deletion request process
- Data export process
- Who can access reports
- Who can access audit logs
- Whether any data leaves Ghana
- Subprocessors or hosting providers

## Privacy Policy Must Explain

- What the app does
- What data is collected
- Why it is collected
- Who can see it
- How long it is kept
- How a user can request deletion
- How safety-sensitive reports are protected
- Support contact

## Store Review Risk Points

- Do not imply official government status unless there is a real agreement.
- Explain responder verification clearly.
- Avoid publishing exact location for sensitive reports.
- Keep privacy policy URL live before store submission.
