Responder playbook

Use this sequence after a serious case is created.

1
Protect life firstCall emergency services for fire, injury, live wires, violent danger, or trapped people before any civic escalation.
2
Send the briefCopy the generated SMS/WhatsApp note to the right assembly, utility desk, volunteer group, or supervisor.
3
Track the clockUpdate status when dispatched, acknowledged, fixed, or blocked. Export the log for public accountability.
4
Close with proofMark resolved only after a return photo, contact confirmation, or field verification.
