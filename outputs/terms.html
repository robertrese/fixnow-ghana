FixNow Ghana Terms and Community Rules

Effective date: July 15, 2026

FixNow Ghana is not currently accepting public production reports. It does not replace emergency services, law enforcement, utility providers, medical professionals, or disaster-response agencies.

Acceptable use

  • Submit reports in good faith about real community service, safety, infrastructure, disaster, health access, sanitation, water, power, road, and public-risk issues.
  • Include only the information needed to verify and route a case.
  • Collect photos, GPS, names, and phone numbers only when safe and permitted.

Not allowed

  • Threats, harassment, hate speech, explicit content, revenge reports, bullying, or targeted abuse.
  • False reports, impersonation, political intimidation, or reports meant to shame private people.
  • Unnecessary images of children, medical details, private addresses, or personal identifiers.
  • Criminal accusations unless local law enforcement involvement is active and legally appropriate.

Moderation

Moderation and public-reporting processes are not active for this build. The owner must publish verified moderation, retention, deletion, and escalation procedures before public launch.

Emergency safety

For life-threatening danger, use the verified emergency contacts in FixNow first. Do not enter floodwater, fire zones, unsafe roads, electrical hazards, violent scenes, or unstable structures to create a report.
